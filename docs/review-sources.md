# Google Maps customer review excerpts

Source: https://maps.app.goo.gl/fFeFDjb3yCZ6AY5c8

The shop listing was checked manually through the user's connected Brave browser on 25 October 2026 (25 ตุลาคม 2569). At that time Google Maps showed a 5.0/5 rating from 14 reviews. These values are a dated snapshot, not a live feed.

The three displayed reviewers each gave five stars:

- Arisa Aekchin: “ช่างทำงานดีมืออาชีพมาก งานออกมาสวย” — excerpt from a longer review.
- อัจฉรี ศรีสุวรรณวงค์: “คุยง่ายลงมือด้วยตัวเอง ทำงานไวละเอียด ปราณีต รอบคอบ ทีมงานดีเก็บงานเรียบร้อย” — excerpt; the original spelling “ปราณีต” is preserved.
- Mayuree Mungnimit: “สั่งได้ท้วงได้ คุยง่ายสบายๆแนะนำเลยค่ะ” — whitespace normalized from the original, which contained zero-width characters.

Relative review ages and edited timestamps are omitted because they become stale. The website labels these as excerpts, displays the snapshot date, and links all source controls to the original business listing. Individual review URLs were not returned by the source's share flow, so no individual links are fabricated. Initials are used instead of recreated reviewer portraits.

No `Review` or `AggregateRating` structured data is emitted. To refresh the snapshot, check the Google Maps listing again and update the central data and checked date in `src/lib/reviews.ts` together.
